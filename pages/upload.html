How to upload (read first)
  1. Upload the Inventory Report first for the month. Clinic Movement needs it for selling prices.
  2. Then upload the other files for that month in any order: Clinic Movement, Shopee MY and SG Sales, Listing Prices.
  3. Always choose the Report type yourself. Do not use "Detect automatically" for Shopee files, or MY can be saved as SG.
  4. Press Preview. Nothing is saved until you tick the box and confirm.
  5. Saved the wrong file? Use 🗑 Delete in "Recent Supabase imports" at the bottom, then upload again.

Upload a report

Select the reporting month, choose the original Excel file, and check the proposed changes. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Maximum 35 MB. Original Shopee performance and mass-update exports are supported.

Only used for Clinic Movement. Clinic Movement has units sold but no prices, so revenue = units sold × the Price column of an Avixo Inventory Report.
Historical: use the Inventory Report of that month (exported on the last day or the 1st of next month). Use this when you already uploaded that month's Inventory Report.
Use latest: use the newest Inventory Report for every product. Revenue is only an estimate because prices may have changed. Use this only when that month has no Inventory Report.
Prices always come from the Price column, never Cost Price.
