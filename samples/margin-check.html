How it is worked out
GM = (price − cost) ÷ price

After fee = (price × (1 − fee) − cost) ÷ price = GM − fee

Price needed = cost ÷ (1 − target) or cost ÷ (1 − fee − target)

Cost is the latest Avixo cost per unit (snapshot of 1 Nov 2026). Price is the latest Shopee MY listing price in the dashboard. The fee is one flat percentage you set, with vouchers at zero, so the after-fee column is an estimate. Shopee's real deductions vary by category, programme and SST; check them in Seller Centre under My Income.
